Feature concept · Halal investors

Zakat, worked out

Your Zakat across Invest, Trade and Save in about a minute, counted the way you follow.

The problem

Sarwa's Halal investors pay Zakat every year, but today they copy balances out of three products, guess how shares should count, look up the gold price and do the 2.5% in a spreadsheet. Nothing in the app helps.

Why it fits Sarwa

Sarwa already runs a Halal portfolio, Save Halal and a Shariah Screener, and already holds every number this needs. Ramadan gives a yearly moment when people want this answer, and a help-centre search finds no Zakat tool.

How it works

  • A seasonal card on Home ("Ramadan is in N days") and a permanent item under Account.
  • Holdings arrive already filled in; switch any off, and pick full market value or the 30% proxy for long-term shares.
  • Add bank cash, gold and money owed to you, then deduct short-term debts.
  • Choose a gold or silver nisab, see whether you're above it, and get 2.5% in USD and AED with a hawl reminder.

Frontend notes

  • One pure computeZakat(inputs) function feeds every screen, so the maths is unit-tested once and the UI only renders derived state.
  • RTL comes from logical CSS properties (inset-inline-start, padding-inline); figures sit in <bdi> so "$" and digits never reorder.
  • Switches use role="switch"; the running total is an aria-live region; focus moves to each screen's heading.
  • Holdings load per product, so one product failing shows its own error and retry without blocking the rest.
