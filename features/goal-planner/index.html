Goals & What-If Planner

Your accounts already have names. Give them a finish line.

The problem

Investors name accounts after what they're saving for ("New House 🏠"), but the app can't say how much, by when, or whether today's auto-deposit gets them there. The only planning tool is a retirement calculator on the website.

Why it fits Sarwa

  • Named accounts, auto-deposits (weekly, monthly, quarterly) and 1/7–7/7 risk levels already exist, so a goal is three new fields on an account.
  • The one-tap fix ends in the existing auto-deposit flow, so the feature pays for itself in net deposits.
  • Wealthfront Path and Betterment goals have it. Sarwa doesn't yet.

How it works

  • Invest Home shows a progress bar and an On track or Behind chip on every account with a goal.
  • Set a goal: target, date, starting balance, deposit and frequency, portfolio and risk level.
  • The What-if planner draws pessimistic, expected and optimistic paths against the target. Every slider updates the chart, the probability and the fees as you drag.
  • If you're behind, it works out the smallest deposit that puts you back on track, and you confirm it as an auto-deposit with the AED amount shown.

Frontend notes

  • The projection is closed-form: exact mean and variance of the balance month by month, fitted to a lognormal. 360 months take under 1 ms, so no Monte Carlo and no worker are needed.
  • One plain state object feeds every screen. Sliders write to it and one render() draws the result; in React this is a reducer plus memoised selectors.
  • Sliders carry aria-valuetext. The chart takes focus and scrubs with the arrow keys, and its label reads out the outcome.
  • RTL uses logical CSS properties. The chart stays in an LTR block so time runs left to right, as finance charts do in Arabic apps too.
