Sarwa Trade concept

Passive Income Calendar

See when your dividends land, what you keep after tax, and what they pay for.

Read the pitch

The problem

Sarwa Trade users can reinvest dividends and browse a "Dividend" theme, but nothing tells them when a payout lands, how much it will be after US withholding, or which date they must hold a stock through to get paid. Market Alerts only fire on "significant" moves, so there's no way to say "tell me when KO hits $65".

Why it fits Sarwa

It turns two features Sarwa already has (dividend reinvesting and the Dividend list) into a reason to open the app every month. Showing net income after the 30% US withholding that UAE residents usually face is the honest, local answer that US apps skip. "Your dividends cover your Salik commute" is plain-English money talk in Sarwa's voice.

How it works

  • A card on the Trade summary shows this month's estimated income and a 12-month sparkline.
  • A month calendar marks ex-dividend dates (ring) and pay dates (amount); a 12-month bar view shows the year. One Net/Gross toggle drives every figure.
  • Each holding shows yield, frequency, next ex-date, a custom price-target alert and the existing "Reinvest dividends" setting.
  • "What it covers" maps monthly net income to UAE costs in AED; a SCHD slider shows what an extra deposit adds.

Frontend notes

  • One incomeSchedule(positions, window) selector feeds the card, grid, bars and coverage, in integer cents, so every total adds up. Net vs gross is a view flag, not a second dataset.
  • The calendar is a roving-tabindex grid: arrow keys move across days and months, each day has a spoken label, and ex vs pay differ by shape, not only colour.
  • Charts are CSS flex bars, not canvas, so dir="rtl" mirrors them for free; layout uses logical properties throughout.
  • The slider re-renders only its results region, so focus and drag survive; dividend data would be cached per symbol and invalidated on each new declaration.
